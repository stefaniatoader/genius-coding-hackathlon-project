Extreme Heat

There are several ways to define extreme heat. It is frequently based on how unusually hot and humid it is for an area.1 While summertime temperatures of 100˚F(37˚C) might be normal for Phoenix, Arizona, they would be considered extreme for Seattle, Washington.
